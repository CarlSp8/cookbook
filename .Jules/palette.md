## 2025-10-26 - [Branding WebRTC Components]
**Learning:** The `fastrtc.WebRTC` component supports `icon`, `icon_button_color`, and `pulse_color` arguments, allowing for easy branding of audio interfaces (e.g. Gemini Sparkle).
**Action:** When implementing audio/video streaming interfaces, leverage these arguments to apply consistent branding (e.g. Gemini colors) instead of leaving the default generic UI.

## 2025-10-26 - [Modernizing Voice UI]
**Learning:** Migrating from deprecated `gradio-webrtc` to `fastrtc` not only fixes compatibility issues but unlocks modern UI customization options like custom icons and pulse colors, which significantly improves the "micro-UX" of voice interactions.
**Action:** Always check for `fastrtc` availability when working with Gradio voice interfaces to leverage improved styling capabilities.
